Good questions

How do I get my client list out of Jobber?

Jobber's client export doesn't include prices, because prices live on your recurring jobs. Use the Recurring Jobs report instead: Insights, then Reports, then Recurring jobs, then Export to CSV. Jobber emails you the file. Load it here and the columns are matched for you.

Can it change the prices inside Jobber for me?

Not yet. Jobber's help pages don't describe a way to update existing recurring job prices by import, so we won't pretend. You get a checklist of exactly which clients to edit, to what price and from which date.

Where does my client list go?

Nowhere. Everything runs in your browser. Your work is kept on this computer, and you can save it as a project file to open again later.

Will I lose clients?

Some owners lose one or two. The churn check shows how many could leave before the raise stops paying off, so you can choose a number you're comfortable with. A kind note sent early makes the biggest difference.
