Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026. In short: your client list is read in your browser and never uploaded.

What stays in your browser

  • The client list you open, your price rules, your letters and the replies you track are saved in this browser’s local storage (keys price-raise-v1 and pr-theme), so the tool remembers them next time.
  • CSV and Excel files are read in your browser. The file itself is never sent anywhere.
  • Everything you export (mail-merge CSV, price-change checklist, calendar file, project file, print-out) is made in your browser and saved to your own computer.
  • Letters are written from fixed templates in your browser. No AI service sees your clients.

What leaves your browser

  • Nothing you enter. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.
  • Excel files (.xlsx or .xls) are read by the SheetJS reader, which comes from this site like the rest of the page. Your file never leaves your browser.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the normal request details when you load a page, and GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Deleting your details

Clear this site’s data in your browser settings (site data or cookies for this address). That removes everything the tool saved. Files you exported are yours to keep or delete.

Not legal advice

Price Raise helps you work out and announce a price rise. It is not legal advice. Check your client agreements and any local rules on notice periods before you send.
