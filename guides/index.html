Price Raise guides

Plain-English guides to raising prices on recurring clients: telling them, and changing the prices in Xero, Jobber and QuickBooks Online, each built on the software’s own help pages.

  • How to tell clients about a price increase (with message templates)
    Tell each client yourself, before the bill changes, with the new amount and the date. The five parts of a good notice, three short templates, and the sum that says how many clients you can lose.
  • How to raise prices on all your Xero repeating invoices
    Xero’s guide has no bulk price change for repeating invoices, so each template is edited once. The order that works, the item-price trap and the Next Invoice Date trap.
  • How to raise prices on recurring jobs in Jobber
    In Jobber the price sits on each recurring job. Why the client export, the client mass update and the job import can’t change it, and how the Recurring Jobs Report gives you the list to work through.
  • How to raise prices on all your QuickBooks Online recurring invoices
    QuickBooks Online can push a new item price into every recurring template that uses the item, with one checkbox. What to do when the checkbox is missing, and how to check it worked before the next invoices go out.
