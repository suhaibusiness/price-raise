Price Raise guides

Plain-English guides to raising prices on recurring clients: telling them, sample price increase letters for cleaning, lawn care, pool service, window cleaning and dog grooming, and changing the prices in Xero, Jobber and QuickBooks Online, each built on the software’s own help pages.

  • How to tell clients about a price increase (with message templates)
    Tell each client yourself, before the bill changes, with the new amount and the date. The five parts of a good notice, three short templates, and the sum that says how many clients you can lose.
  • Price increase letter for a cleaning business (sample letter and notice guide)
    A full sample price increase letter for recurring house cleaning clients, how much notice to give, what to say when a client pushes back, and the sum that tells you how many clients you can afford to lose.
  • Price increase letter for a lawn care business (sample letter and notice guide)
    A full sample price increase letter for lawn mowing and lawn care clients, when to send it in the season, what to say when a client pushes back, and how many lawns you can lose and still come out ahead.
  • Price increase letter for a pool service business (sample letter and notice guide)
    A full sample price increase letter for weekly pool service clients, how much notice to give, why shorter letters land better, what to do when a client pushes back, and how many pools you can lose and still come out ahead.
  • Price increase letter for a window cleaning business (sample letter and notice guide)
    A full sample price increase letter for repeat window cleaning clients, when to tell them on a round, how big a rise to make in one go, what to say when a client pushes back, and the sum behind it.
  • Price increase notice for a dog grooming business (sample letter and wording)
    A full sample price increase notice for regular dog grooming clients, when to tell them, wording that doesn’t apologise, what to do when a client pushes back, and how many regulars you can lose and still come out ahead.
  • How to raise prices on all your Xero repeating invoices
    Xero’s guide has no bulk price change for repeating invoices, so each template is edited once. The order that works, the item-price trap and the Next Invoice Date trap.
  • How to raise prices on recurring jobs in Jobber
    In Jobber the price sits on each recurring job. Why the client export, the client mass update and the job import can’t change it, and how the Recurring Jobs Report gives you the list to work through.
  • How to raise prices on all your QuickBooks Online recurring invoices
    QuickBooks Online can push a new item price into every recurring template that uses the item, with one checkbox. What to do when the checkbox is missing, and how to check it worked before the next invoices go out.
