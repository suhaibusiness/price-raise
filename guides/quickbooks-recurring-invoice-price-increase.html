Raising prices in QuickBooks Online

How to raise prices on all your QuickBooks Online recurring invoices

Change the item’s price under Products and services and tick “Update price in recurring template, too”. QuickBooks then updates every recurring template that uses that item. If the box isn’t there, Intuit says to change the price in a template by hand first, and the box then appears.

Change the price once, for every template

  1. Select Settings ⚙, then Products and services.
  2. Find the item and select Edit.
  3. In Sales price/rate, enter the new price.
  4. Tick Update price in recurring template, too.
  5. Select Save and close.

“Update an item price to all recurring templates it appears on at once.”

QuickBooks Help: Update item prices in recurring templates, read 12 November 2026

When the checkbox isn’t there

Intuit’s fix: open one recurring template that uses the item, change the item’s price there by hand to the new price, save it, then go back to the item. The checkbox should now show.

One owner with more than 700 templates found the box gone altogether. So don’t assume it worked: check.

“…manually change the item price in the recurring template to match the new item price…”

QuickBooks Help, on the missing checkbox, read 12 November 2026

Check it worked before the next invoices

  1. Go to Settings, then Recurring transactions.
  2. Open three or four templates for different clients and look at the item’s price in Item details.
  3. If one still shows the old price, select Edit in the Action column, change the price and select Save template.
  4. After the next run, open Reports, search for the Recent Automatic Transactions report and check a few of the new invoices.

“These changes only apply to the recurring template and transactions that use the template.”

QuickBooks Help: Edit a recurring template, read 12 November 2026

Do it in this order

  1. Decide each client’s new price and the date it starts.
  2. Tell each client before the first invoice at the new price.
  3. Change the prices after the last invoice at the old price has been created, and before the next one.

A Scheduled template can be set to create its invoice some days ahead, so the change has to land before that.
