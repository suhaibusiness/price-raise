Raising prices in Xero

How to raise prices on all your Xero repeating invoices

Xero’s own guide gives no way to change prices on many repeating invoices at once: you edit each template. An edit applies to every invoice that template creates afterwards, so it’s one edit per client, done once, at the right moment.

Changing the item price isn’t enough

If you change an inventory item’s selling price, repeating invoices keep the old one. Xero says the price then has to be changed on each invoice after it’s created. Editing the template instead fixes every invoice after it.

“…the updated price won’t flow through to new repeating invoices…”

Xero Central, on changing an item’s price, read 4 November 2026

Edit a template

  1. In the Sales menu, select Invoices.
  2. Open the Repeating tab. Each template shows the date of its next invoice.
  3. Click the template and change the price on its lines.
  4. Click Save. Xero records the change in History & Notes.

“…your changes are applied to all subsequent transactions…”

Xero Central: Add or edit a repeating invoice template, read 4 November 2026

Do it in this order

  1. Decide each client’s new price and the date it starts.
  2. Tell each client before the first invoice at the new price.
  3. Edit each template after its last invoice at the old price has been created, and before the next one.
  4. Tick each template off as you go, so none is missed or done twice.

A template set to Approve for Sending emails the invoice on the day it’s created, so its edit has to land before that date. One set to Save As Draft gives you a chance to check the draft first.

The trap: don’t move the Next Invoice Date into the past

If a template has already created invoices and you set its Next Invoice Date to a past date, Xero creates every invoice again from that date. You’d then have to delete or void the originals. Change the price, not the date.

“…all transactions are created again from that date…”

Xero Central, on the Next Invoice Date, read 4 November 2026

Example

One line per template, so nothing is missed:

Green Lawn Co.   Monthly lawn care   $120.00 -> $132.00   notice sent 1 Dec   edit after the 1 Jan invoice   [ ]
Rivera Dental    Weekly office clean  $260.00 -> $286.00   notice sent 1 Dec   edit after the 1 Jan invoice   [ ]
